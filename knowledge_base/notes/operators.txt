OPERATORS - Study Notes

WHAT ARE OPERATORS?
Operators perform operations on variables.

ARITHMETIC
+ addition
- subtraction
* multiplication
/ division
% modulus
** exponent

EXAMPLE
  a = 5 + 2  # 7

COMPARISON
==, !=, >, <, >=, <=

LOGICAL
and, or, not

EXAMPLE
  if x > 5 and x < 10:
      print("Valid")

COMMON MISTAKES
- mixing types incorrectly
- misunderstanding operator precedence

WHEN TO USE
Use operators to perform calculations and comparisons.

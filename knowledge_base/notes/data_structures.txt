DATA STRUCTURES - Study Notes

LISTS
- Ordered, mutable, allows duplicates
- my_list = [1, 2, 3]
- Access: my_list[0] gives 1
- Append: my_list.append(4)
- Slice: my_list[1:3] gives [2, 3]

TUPLES
- Ordered, immutable
- my_tuple = (1, 2, 3)
- Cannot modify after creation
- Use for fixed data

DICTIONARIES
- Key-value pairs
- my_dict = {'name': 'Alice', 'age': 20}
- Access: my_dict['name'] gives 'Alice'
- Keys must be unique and immutable

SETS
- Unordered, no duplicates
- my_set = {1, 2, 3}
- Operations: union |, intersection &, difference -

STACKS (using list)
- LIFO: Last In First Out
- Push: stack.append(item)
- Pop: stack.pop()

QUEUES (using collections.deque)
- FIFO: First In First Out
- Enqueue: queue.append(item)
- Dequeue: queue.popleft()

BIG-O COMPLEXITY
- List access: O(1)
- List search: O(n)
- Dict access: O(1) average
- Dict search: O(1) average
